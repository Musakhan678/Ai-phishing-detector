        Technical details

        These are separate text-matching checks, not the AI prediction. We have not checked the sender, links, or websites.

        TF-IDF turns the email's words and short phrases into numbers that describe their importance. A trained Linear SVM combines those numbers using patterns learned from labeled phishing/scam and legitimate emails.

        An ordinary word such as “package” may push toward phishing, while “password” may push toward legitimate. The model learned statistical associations from its training emails, not universal rules about those words. A password request is not safe just because a word pushes toward legitimate.

        The final prediction combines many text features and the model's baseline, a starting score learned during training. Features can push in opposite directions. The selected patterns above are only part of this calculation, not probabilities or proof of safety.
